# Product Vision

## Product
**Kingdom Chronicles: Explore the World. Discover the Word.**

A game-first bilingual Bible adventure for teens and Bible college beginners/intermediates. It must feel like a polished, interactive cartoon adventure with attractive animations—not a traditional study platform.

## Core player loop
Explore -> inspect -> converse -> solve -> witness a scripted biblical event -> discover a reliable Scripture reference -> unlock journal connections -> continue exploring.

## Differentiator
The **Living Scripture Journal** connects characters, events, locations and related passages into an explorable knowledge graph. Knowledge is acquired by curiosity and interactions, not by mandatory lessons.

## Audience
Christian teens, youth groups and Bible college beginners/intermediates. Both Sinhala and English are first-class experiences. Design mobile-browser controls from day one.

## Design principles
1. Make exploration intrinsically fun.
2. Make biblical meaning central, not a decorative theme.
3. Preserve canonical narrative outcomes.
4. Make source references visible; clarify where fictional game navigation starts.
5. Build reusable scene and mission systems, not one-off code per story.
6. Respect time, devices, and accessibility needs.
7. No gambling, predatory streaks, manipulative monetization, unrestricted player chat, or unreviewed theological AI chats in MVP.

## MVP
First milestone: a beautiful responsive ancient Israelite village with playable movement, NPC dialogue, camera, collisions, an investigation clue and a journal entry. Next: a full David and Goliath adventure (1 Samuel 17).

## Long-term
Build indexed coverage of the 66-book Protestant canon, including narratives, poetry, wisdom, law, prophecy and letters. Not all passages become action-game stories: the interactive journal and themed investigations cover non-narrative content. Expansion depends on content licensing and human theological review.

## Success criteria
Players can enter the world, understand controls without coaching, complete the first mission, find linked passages, and remember what the story actually says. Enjoyment and factual accuracy matter more than XP.
